# Embryo Sim

Embryo Sim is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Verilator](https://github.com/verilator/verilator) (654d6a6, commit 654d6a6). It turns chip code into a fast simulator.
It is one of the Embryo chip tools, with Embryo Router (our Freerouting fork) and Embryo PCB (our KiCad fork).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, LICENSES) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
